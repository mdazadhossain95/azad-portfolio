# Four Portfolio Directions

## V1 — Classic Developer

Purpose: recruiter-friendly technical portfolio.

Remembered for: precise typography, numbered navigation, experience tabs, restrained Flutter cyan.

Avoid: cursor glow, cyber effects, excessive cards, generic SaaS sections.

## V2 — FinTech Systems Lab

Purpose: demonstrate FinTech, payments, secure workflows, APIs, and production systems.

Remembered for: credible systems map and structured project records.

Avoid: hacker language, Matrix effects, fake live financial data, crypto hype, gaming UI.

## V3 — Engineering Notebook

Purpose: show personality, process, and creative engineering thinking.

Remembered for: warm paper, restrained hand-drawn annotations, polaroid portrait, product sketches.

Avoid: handwriting for paragraphs, childish doodles, overlapping essential content, low contrast.

## V4 — Mobile Product Engineer

Purpose: canonical client-conversion portfolio.

Remembered for: premium mobile product presentation, real phone screenshots, strong case studies, subtle cyan pointer light.

Avoid: fake app screens, blocking splash screens, unsupported trust metrics, excessive parallax.

## Shared rules

- Use one verified content source.
- Keep V4 canonical at `/`.
- Keep V1–V3 `noindex, follow`.
- Share case-study routes.
- Do not copy reference-site source code, text, logo, or distinctive branded assets.
